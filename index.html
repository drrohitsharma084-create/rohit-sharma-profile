About the doctor

Care that puts patients first.

Dr. Rohit Sharma is a General Physician focused on the assessment and management of a range of common medical concerns. His approach emphasizes listening to patients, evaluating symptoms carefully, explaining treatment options and supporting better day-to-day health.

Every consultation should be guided by respect, clear communication and attention to each patient's individual needs.
